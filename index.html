Install Wired

Pick your device. After installing, Wired updates itself: press Update when it says a new version is ready.

Wired for TVFire TV, Google TV, Android TV Wired for AndroidPhones and tablets Wired for WindowsUnzip, then open wired.exe

Signing in

  • Phone / PC: Settings → Sign in with the family email and password, then pick your profile.
  • TV: Wired shows a code. On a signed-in phone: Settings → Account → Sign in a TV.

If something stops you

  • Windows "protected your PC": More info → Run anyway.
  • Android "install unknown apps": allow it for your browser or Downloader, then Install.
  • Fire TV: Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps → Downloader → On.
